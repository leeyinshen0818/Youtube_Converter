YouTube to MP3 Batch Converter

Send this single file:
  YoutubeConverter.exe

Run:
  YoutubeConverter.exe

The app opens your browser at:
  http://127.0.0.1:8080

Keep the small black app window open while converting. Close it to stop the app.

Converted MP3 files are saved in:
  downloads

FFmpeg is bundled inside the app.

Use this only for videos you own, public-domain content, Creative Commons content, or material you have permission to download.
